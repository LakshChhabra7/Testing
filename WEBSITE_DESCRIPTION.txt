THERMOSHELTER AI — AREA-SPECIFIC PASSIVE SHELTER THERMAL DESIGN & SIMULATION PLATFORM
================================================================================

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THERMOSHELTER AI (TherMusk) is an advanced web-based engineering and architectural
simulation application designed for modeling, evaluating, and optimizing passive 
bioclimatic shelters across extreme and diverse climate zones. 

The platform bridges architectural design with numerical thermodynamic modeling, 
allowing engineers, architects, researchers, and field disaster-relief planners 
to construct high-efficiency shelters that maintain internal thermal comfort with 
minimal or zero active HVAC energy consumption.


2. CORE MODULES & FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 3D DIGITAL TWIN & DESIGN STUDIO:
  - Real-time 3D WebGL (Three.js) viewport featuring realistic geometries, 
    sun-path trajectory simulation, and customized environmental sky background.
  - Multi-view inspection modes: Physical Architectural view, False-Color Thermal 
    Infrared (IR) gradients, Incident Solar Rays, Airflow streamline stack-effect 
    particles, and Exploded Layer Disassembly.
  - Parametric controls for shelter dimensions (Length, Width, Height), azimuth 
    orientation (True South solar alignment), window-to-wall ratios (WWR) by facade, 
    shading overhangs, and airtightness (ACH).

* TRANSIENT THERMAL SOLVER (Euler-RK4 ODE):
  - Numerical resolution of first-law thermodynamic energy balance equations.
  - Hourly 24-hour diurnal cycle calculations computing ambient outdoor temperature, 
    sol-air surface temperatures, direct solar fenestration gain, envelope conduction 
    heat loss, ventilation heat exchange, and ASHRAE 55 PMV thermal comfort index.

* EVOLUTIONARY MULTI-OBJECTIVE OPTIMIZATION ENGINE:
  - Genetic Pareto solver exploring high-dimensional parametric design spaces.
  - Optimizes orientation, wall composite thickness, thermal mass, and glazing ratios 
    against objectives: Balanced Net-Zero, Maximum Thermal Comfort, Minimum Heat Loss, 
    or Maximum Solar Harvest.

* MATERIALS LABORATORY & COMPOSITE ENVELOPE BUILDER:
  - Database of regional building materials (Rammed Earth, CSEB, AAC Blocks, PUF/PIR 
    Insulation, Rockwool, Aerogel, Timber, Straw Bale, Reflective Foil).
  - Calculates Mackey-Wright periodic thermal admittance, total thermal resistance 
    (R-value), overall transmittance (U-value), volumetric heat capacity, decrement 
    damping factor (f), and diurnal thermal time lag (phase delay in hours).

* CLIMATE HUB:
  - Dedicated meteorological profiles for 6 diverse bioclimatic regions across India:
    1. Leh, Ladakh (Cold-Arid Extreme)
    2. Jaisalmer, Rajasthan (Hot-Arid Desert)
    3. New Delhi (Composite Plains)
    4. Lucknow (Subtropical Humid)
    5. Bengaluru (Moderate Plateau)
    6. Chennai (Warm-Humid Coastal)

* SYNTHETIC DATASET EXPLORER (228 SCENARIOS):
  - Interactive database containing 228 pre-computed parametric shelter simulations.
  - Search, filter by climate archetype, inspect engineering metrics, and export to Excel/CSV.

* BENCHMARK COMPARATOR & DESIGN REPORTS:
  - Side-by-side comparative analytics contrasting uninsulated baseline structures 
    against bioclimatic optimized shelters.
  - Automated printable engineering specification reports detailing heat loss 
    breakdowns, passive solar fraction, embodied carbon metrics, and compliance data.

* ANSYS / CFD ADAPTER:
  - Generates ready-to-run ANSYS APDL scripts and Python OpenFOAM setup code to export 
    shelter geometries and boundary conditions directly into industrial FEA/CFD packages.


3. TECHNOLOGY STACK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Frontend Framework: React 19 + TypeScript
- Build Tool: Vite
- Styling & UI: TailwindCSS (with glassmorphism and sky/cloud theme tokens)
- 3D Graphics Engine: Three.js (WebGL 60FPS digital twin)
- Data Visualization: Recharts (Dynamic diurnal temperature and heat flow graphs)
- Icons: Lucide-React
- Data Processing: SheetJS (XLSX)
